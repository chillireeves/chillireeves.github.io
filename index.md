## About / 关于

I teach English language and linguistics at CQUE, with research interests at the intersection of **corpus linguistics**, **academic / professional English (EAP/ESP)**&#8203;, and **generative-AI–enhanced language teaching**. My current work centres on corpus-assisted discourse studies (CADS), technology-supported feedback in second-language writing, and the phonological / discourse features of Chongqing dialect in L2 English learning.

主要承担英语语言学与英语教学法课程。研究方向集中于**语料库辅助话语研究（CADS）**&#8203;、**生成式 AI 与数字技术赋能二语写作**，以及**重庆方言对英语语音习得的负迁移效应**。
